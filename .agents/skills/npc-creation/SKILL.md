# NPC Creation

Use `dm-hero-authoring` for campaign resolution, proposals, approval, writes, and canon safety. Use `fantasy-narrative-style-skill` for character pressure, emotional causality, and dialogue voice.

An NPC biography alone is insufficient. The saved NPC needs at least one approved campaign-grounded relationship or an approved linked world addition.

Search matching names and roles, then inspect relevant Factions, Locations, Lore, Quests, Dialogues, Manuscript scenes, and NPC relations. Prefer an existing entity when it already represents the character.

Every proposal includes role/current pressure, want, fear, tactic, contradiction, cost, formative history, public face, private truth, knowledge limits, concrete world ties, campaign/main-character effect, metadata, tags/folder, optional portrait/document, and every invented fact marked proposed.

If grounding is missing, propose the smallest useful Lore, Faction, Location, Quest, or relationship addition separately. After approval, create/update the NPC, create relations/links, verify campaign ownership and duplicates, and report the NPC ID/UID, world links, canon status, and unresolved assumptions.
