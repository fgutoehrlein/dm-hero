# Manuscript Writing

Use `dm-hero-authoring` for campaign resolution, proposals, approval, writes, and canon safety. Use `fantasy-narrative-style-skill` for fantasy scenes, pacing, foreshadowing, dialogue, and emotional arcs.

Maintain the campaign Manuscript as the main-character book using book → part → chapter → scene, ordered beats, Markdown prose, narrative links, lifecycle/canon state, and revisions.

1. Read the Manuscript and connected NPCs, Factions, Locations, Lore, Quests, and Dialogues.
2. Establish protagonist goal, pressure, emotional movement, and canon constraints.
3. Propose hierarchy and beats before prose: purpose, POV, setting, conflict, change, and exit question.
4. Wait for outline approval, then create/reorder only the approved structure.
5. Draft or revise scenes as Markdown proposals; preserve user facts and voice.
6. After prose approval, write, link references, and verify backlinks/revisions.

Outline approval does not authorize prose. Keep chronology, knowledge, quest state, and consequences consistent. Report section IDs, order, word-count change, links, revisions, unresolved links, and canon warnings.
